# Relabelling with an independent M/X association and walk-forward rerun (2026-11-11)

## Decision

Reading R1 of the [frozen protocol](../config/relabel-v1.json) holds: the missing cycle-25 labels were a main
cause of the cycle-25 weakness. Of the 622 HEK M/X events that SWAN-SF V2 attached to no HARP, 489 (132 in 2022,
269 in 2023) are attached to exactly one HARP by the NOAA SGAS region number joined to the JSOC `NOAA_ARS`
keyword. Rebuilding the development table from the verified V2 archives with these events brings back 63304
windows that the fail-closed rule had excluded, 5899 of them positive; every one of the 595671 frozen windows
keeps identical features and its frozen status. On the relabelled table the refitted frozen recipes reach, at the
recall-0.90 operating point, precision 0.1452 in 2022 (frozen: 0.0442; bootstrap interval [0.1002, 0.1969]) and
0.1874 in 2023 (frozen: 0.1186; [0.1518, 0.2232]); average precision rises from 0.2800 to 0.4622 and from 0.2206
to 0.3626, into the cycle-24 range. The goal is not reached: no year of any arm has precision 0.50 or accuracy at
or above always-negative (R2: zero years). The frozen models scored on the new labels (label effect alone) show
higher precision than the refit in 2022 and 2023 (R3), but at recall 0.9123 and 0.7480 with thresholds chosen on the
old labels, so the two are not the same operating point. The relabelled table is development evidence; the
production model, the operating point and the dashboard are unchanged; the reserved 2024–2025 test was not read.
The protocol was frozen and committed before any result.

## Why this protocol

The [label coverage audit](label-coverage-20261110.md) found that only 29.6% (2022) and 28.9% (2023) of the
`NOAA_ARS`-mapped SGAS M/X flares reach a positive label, against 87–95% in 2014–2017, because V2 attached 174 and
293 events of those years to no HARP and the windows containing them were excluded. Flaring HARPs kept only
negative windows in training and evaluation.

## Method

Association: each V2-unassigned HEK event is matched to the SGAS section-A rows with the same GOES class and a
peak time within 5 minutes; the matched region numbers are looked up in `NOAA_ARS` on the SGAS peak date. One
candidate HARP: `recovered`, attached at the HEK peak time. Several: `ambiguous`, windows of every candidate HARP
containing the peak are excluded. No HARP, no region or no SGAS match: the event stays unassigned and keeps
excluding windows as before. V2's own attachments are never overridden. The table is rebuilt from the four
verified V2 archives with the frozen roles, stride, cadence and leap-second rules, then checked window by window
against the frozen shards. The walk-forward recipes of `config/walk-forward-v1.json` (expanding training,
previous-year calibration, 0.45 logistic + 0.55 XGBoost, sigmoid calibration) are refitted on the relabelled
table (`relabelled_models`); the frozen per-year models and thresholds are also applied to the relabelled
evaluation windows (`frozen_models_new_labels`); the frozen run on the frozen labels is the reference
(`frozen_models_old_labels`). Operating points: previous-year calibration threshold at window recall 0.95 (the
frozen walk-forward rule) and 0.90 (the operating-point-v2 watch rule). Intervals are 95% HARP-cluster bootstraps
(1000 draws, seed 24).

## Reading

- R1 holds: in 2022 and 2023 the relabelled-model precision at recall 0.90 (0.1452, 0.1874) exceeds the frozen
  value (0.0442, 0.1186) and its interval lower bound (0.1002, 0.1518) lies above it.
- R2: zero years with accuracy at or above always-negative in every arm; precision stays below 0.50 everywhere.
- R3: `frozen_models_new_labels` has the higher precision in 2022 (0.1551) and 2023 (0.2771), at recall 0.9123
  and 0.7480. In 2023 the frozen threshold no longer reaches recall 0.90 on the new labels, so its precision is
  measured at a stricter point; the refit, calibrated on the relabelled previous year, reaches recall 0.9242 and
  0.9032 as intended. Average precision, which needs no threshold, is slightly higher for the refit (0.4622 vs
  0.4497; 0.3626 vs 0.3579).
- Cycle-24 years change little: 2014–2017 precision at recall 0.90 moves within its intervals (2014 0.2082 to
  0.1922, 2015 0.1721 to 0.1473, 2016 0.1106 to 0.1045, 2017 0.1850 to 0.1868) and AP stays within 0.03.

## Limits

SGAS region numbers and `NOAA_ARS` are associations made by NOAA and JSOC, not ground truth; 133 events remain
unassigned (71 without region number, 41 without an SGAS match, 17 unmapped on their date, 4 ambiguous). The
relabelled 2022 and 2023 roles are larger than the frozen ones (65593 and 96991 windows), so the arms are
compared on different window sets; the reference row keeps the frozen set. The result is development evidence on
years already examined many times; adopting the relabelled table for the production model, the operating point or
the dashboard is a separate decision, and the reserved 2024–2025 test was not read.
